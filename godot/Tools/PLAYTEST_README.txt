RISK OF COPYRIGHT - playtest build

Start: unzip the whole folder, then double-click RiskOfCopyright.exe.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway"
(the game isn't code-signed yet).

You start on a dock. Walk to the end of the diving board and press E to dive to the sea floor, then survive the waves.

CONTROLS
  Move                W A S D
  Look                Mouse
  Jump / jetpack      Space (hold in the air to thrust)
  Sprint              Shift
  Crouch / slide      Ctrl (while sprinting to slide)
  Walk                Alt
  Shoot / aim         Left mouse / right mouse
  Reload              R
  Switch weapon       1, 2 or mouse wheel
  Melee (axe)         V or mouse side button
  Surge (dash boost)  Q or mouse side button (hold to charge)
  Use / buy           E
  Third person        C
  Command ally        Z
  Dev menu            Esc, then Dev menu (maps, skins, cheats)
  Pause and settings  Esc (sensitivity, volume, field of view, fullscreen, restart, quit)

CONTROLLER (the game switches by itself to whichever you touched last: pad, or keyboard and mouse)
  Move / look         Left stick / right stick
  Jump / jetpack      A (hold in the air to thrust)
  Crouch / slide      B
  Sprint              Left bumper (or click the left stick)
  Shoot / aim         Right trigger / left trigger
  Reload / use, buy   X / Y
  Melee (axe)         Right bumper
  Surge               Click the right stick (hold to charge)
  Switch weapon       D-pad left / right
  Third person        D-pad up
  Command ally        D-pad down
  Pause               Start (B closes the menu); sticks and D-pad move through it, A picks
  Settings            Stick sensitivity, aim assist and vibration are in the pause menu
  Aim assist          On by default with a pad: the stick slows over an enemy and the aim is drawn toward it
  Dev menu            D-pad or left stick to move, A to press, bumpers change tab, right stick scrolls, B closes;
                      after Spawn, right trigger places the enemy and B cancels

Sounds are placeholders for now. Please note anything that feels off:
jitter, flashing, things stuck in walls, crashes, and how it feels to play.
