RISK OF COPYRIGHT - playtest build

Start: unzip the whole folder, then double-click RiskOfCopyright.exe.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway"
(the game isn't code-signed yet).

You start on a dock. Jump off and dive to the sea floor, then survive the waves.

CONTROLS
  Move                W A S D
  Look                Mouse
  Jump / jetpack      Space (hold in the air to thrust)
  Sprint              Shift
  Crouch / slide      Ctrl (while sprinting to slide)
  Walk                Alt
  Shoot / aim         Left mouse / right mouse
  Reload              R
  Switch weapon       1, 2 or mouse wheel
  Melee (axe)         V or mouse side button
  Surge (dash boost)  Q or mouse side button (hold to charge)
  Use / buy           E
  Third person        C
  Command ally        Z
  Dev menu            P (maps, skins, cheats)
  Free the mouse      Esc

Sounds are placeholders for now. Please note anything that feels off:
jitter, flashing, things stuck in walls, crashes, and how it feels to play.
